FileFlex – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

FileFlex is a file converter that runs in your browser or as an installed web app. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your files stay on your device

FileFlex converts files on your own device. The files you choose are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not receive, store or see them. FileFlex has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics.

What is stored on your device

FileFlex saves a short conversion history (file names, formats and dates), your light or dark mode choice, and a copy of the app and its converter libraries so it can work offline. This is kept in your browser’s storage on your device. You can remove the history with the “Clear History” button, or clear everything by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Downloads from other services

To work, FileFlex loads converter libraries from public content delivery networks (cdnjs by Cloudflare and jsDelivr). Some optional features download extra components the first time you use them:

  • AI person cut-out: downloads a small AI model from Google-hosted and jsDelivr servers.
  • Scanned-PDF text recognition (OCR): downloads the text-recognition engine and English language data.

When your device requests these downloads, those services can see technical information such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies. Your files and their contents are not sent to them.

Children

FileFlex is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
