Quality applies to JPG, WEBP and PDF image output. Width, Height and Scale resize any image. With both Width and Height, Resize mode decides how the picture fits: inside the box, stretched, cropped to fill, or padded with background bars (the last three give exactly that pixel size). Background fills transparent areas; JPG and PDF always get a solid background, and Auto means white for them. Target size makes JPG, WEBP and PNG output at most that many MB by lowering quality, then size (and overrides the quality slider). It is not applied to PDF output. The preview shows the first waiting image with your background settings; tap the original to pick the backdrop colour. “AI person cut-out” finds the person automatically (best for portraits; the first use downloads the AI model, so it needs internet). Choosing White, Black, Custom or Transparent turns on “Replace plain background”, which recolours the backdrop around the picture. “Edge-connected” follows the backdrop in from the edges (it copes with gradients and shadows; raise Tolerance if some is left behind). “A specific colour” replaces every pixel close to the colour you pick (good for a blue passport-photo backdrop). It works on plain or smooth backgrounds and can’t cut a person out of a busy scene.

Your files are converted on your device and never uploaded. Document conversions (PDF, DOCX, TXT, RTF) keep the text only; images, tables and styling are not carried over. History saves only file names and dates in this browser.

About

FileFlex converts files entirely in your browser. Supported: images (JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, GIF → each other or PDF), documents (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, RTF, MD, HTML output), spreadsheets (XLSX, CSV, TSV), subtitles (SRT, VTT, TXT), audio (MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC → WAV or MP3), video audio extraction (MP4, MOV, WebM → MP3 or WAV), plus Merge PDFs, Split pages, Images → one PDF, quality and resize options, and Download ZIP.

Document conversions carry text only (DOCX → HTML keeps basic formatting). PPTX input gives the slide text only; text → PPTX makes one slide per paragraph block; PDF → PPTX makes one picture slide per page (not editable). PDF input needs real text; scanned PDFs are read with OCR (English only, slow and approximate, and it downloads an OCR engine the first time). Text with non-Latin characters is written to PDF as page images, so it can’t be selected or searched. XLSX exports the first sheet. Video-to-video conversion, HEIC/TIFF/AVIF, legacy .doc/.ppt/.xls files and EPUB are not supported here; they need server-side tools. Anything unsupported is rejected, never faked.
